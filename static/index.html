Churn risk dashboard

Finds the telecom customers most likely to leave, explains why, and suggests what to do about it.

Overview Segments Customers Model
Proof of concept on IBM's Telco Customer Churn sample data: a fictional California telco with 7,043 customers (IBM Cognos Analytics base samples, via Kaggle; the source states no licence terms). Figures cover the 1,409 held-out test customers, in US dollars.

Built by Kwame Baafi Boateng · XGBoost, SHAP, FastAPI, plain HTML/CSS/JS · Model from an MSc Business Analytics dissertation (University of Greenwich).
